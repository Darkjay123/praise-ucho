I
Chapter one · The writer

Stories people finish.

I'm Praise Ucho, a writer who turns products, ideas, and complex topics into stories that hold attention and move people to act.

My work runs on one belief: strategy decides what a brand says, and story decides whether anyone listens. I take time to understand the product, the brand, and the people it's meant to reach before I write a word, then refine until the work fits the vision and does its job.

Four years of writing stand behind that claim, two of them scripting stories; the craft has been daily practice long before it became a career.
